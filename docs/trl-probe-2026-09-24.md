## Licence and mineability

**GDELT DOC 2.0.** Published as an open dataset for research and analysis;
the API returns only article titles, source URLs, domains, and metadata —
never article body text — because the full text remains the original
publisher's content, not GDELT's to redistribute. This was directly
confirmed by the probe: every `gdelt-*.json` file in `data/trl/probe/`
contains titles and URLs only. Mining the title/URL stream itself is
permitted; a collector could ingest it directly. Getting to full text for
scoring would require a second, separate fetch of the publisher's own page,
with that publisher's own terms applying — GDELT's openness does not extend
to the linked content.

**GlobeNewswire and PR Newswire.** Both are commercial newswire services
whose business is press-release *distribution* — companies pay to have a
release publicly and widely redistributed, which is the opposite of a
mining restriction. Full release text is present directly on the search
results page (link list) and on each release's own page; both returned
plain HTTP 200 with complete, script-free HTML body text (confirmed by
fetching and reading 34 individual release pages — see
`data/trl/probe/releases/*.txt`). Neither site returned a 403, a
JavaScript-only shell, or a captcha at any point in this probe. The
significant limitation found is not access but scope: their keyword-search
endpoints take no date parameter, so results are not reliably confined to
the trailing 8 weeks (GlobeNewswire's `gs1_2d` results ran from 2018 to
2025; two of the four hand-counted PR Newswire pilot-band items were
outside the 8-week window and had to be excluded above). A real collector
would need either a documented date filter or client-side filtering by the
release's own dateline, which every release page carries in plain text.

## Verdict

**Pass line (per the brief): ≥ 3 pilot-band documents per technology per 8
weeks, applied per (source, technology) pair.**

| source | technologies passing (≥3) | which |
|---|---|---|
| GDELT | 1 of 5 | delivery_drones only |
| GlobeNewswire | 0 of 5 | — |
| PR Newswire | 0 of 5 | — |

No source passes for 3 or more of the 5 technologies. **The plan's condition
— "at least one source passes for at least 3 of 5 technologies" — is not
met.**

The one pass that did occur (GDELT × delivery_drones, 9 hand-counted titles)
is weaker than the raw number suggests: those 9 titles are wire-syndicated
coverage of only 4 distinct underlying events (Amazon Prime Air's footprint
expansion, Lowe's pilot with Wing/DoorDash, DoorDash's own drone-delivery
launch, and an Uber–Zipline partnership). Even at the more conservative
distinct-event count (4), it still clears the ≥3 bar for this one
technology — but it is a single technology out of five, well short of the
plan's 3-of-5 condition regardless of how the duplicates are counted.

Two secondary limitations temper confidence in the zero counts for
`digital_product_passport`, `humanoid_logistics`, and `gs1_2d`: the
`humanoid_logistics` GDELT query (unquoted `"humanoid robot" warehouse`)
drifted toward general humanoid-robot news rather than logistics-specific
deployments, and `gs1_2d`'s query returned very few GDELT results (3) and no
PR Newswire results at all, making its zero a thin-sample result rather than
a confidently-searched absence. A better-tuned query might move these two
technologies' counts up somewhat, but nothing in the sample suggests either
would clear the ≥3 bar, let alone flip the overall verdict.

**Reversal condition:** re-run this probe (or a Task-5 collector) with (a)
corrected, phrase-anchored queries for `humanoid_logistics` and `gs1_2d`,
and (b) either a licensed news source or a genuinely date-filtered
press-release search. If GDELT or a press-release source then clears ≥3
pilot-band documents per technology for at least 3 of 5 technologies over a
trailing 8-week window, Task 5 should add that source's collector. Until
then, Task 5 runs on the existing free-source collectors only (TRL 1–4 /
research-band estimation), and the Task 7 report page should carry the
standing line: "Pilot-band evidence is not collected until a mineable news
source is licensed."

## Concerns for Task 8 / STATUS §8

- GDELT is workable but not casually reliable from this machine today: it
  429'd twice during this session and needed two wait-and-retry cycles. A
  production collector needs backoff tuned for sustained, not just
  per-request, rate limits.
- GlobeNewswire/PR Newswire keyword search is not date-scoped; any future
  collector must filter by each release's own dateline client-side.
- The `humanoid_logistics` and `gs1_2d` queries in this probe likely
  undercount; they are adequate for a go/no-go measurement but not
  production-quality queries.
